## Review concerns that did not justify changing results

The supplied QC reports were treated as claims to check, not as proof. The retained targeted adjudication established:

- Changing 0.800 to 0.801 would substitute the LOF-plus-missense subtotal for the predefined coding cohort; the original 0.800 is correct.
- The reference replay's **3,696** comparisons are named fields/arrays, including 336 diagram arrays: 84 × 43 + 21 × 4. They are not 3,696 independent biological observations.
- The questioned Holm-adjusted values **0.896** and **1** use the complete 16-test sensitivity family and its cumulative step-down maximum. The four-test primary family is distinct.
- Additional leader gains of **43, 42 and 35** at tolerances 0, 1e-6 and 1e-5 reproduce from the retained inputs; the primary result remains 42.
- The discovery and GM12878 replication remainders really are decreases: 82 and 70, respectively, with no exactly unchanged distances in those cohorts.
- The questioned references 2–5 were supported by publisher/registration records in the targeted citation audit. Their metadata were retained, including the explicit awaiting-peer-review qualification where applicable. This does not validate the cited studies' biological conclusions or constitute a new exhaustive citation audit.
- Prior full-cohort local replay exists. Its scope must be distinguished from limited container testing, scalar verification, another PH implementation and independent biological validation. Missing context in a review report is not evidence that no prior checks occurred.
