# Complete companion coverage map

The complete edition covers the frozen 28 October 2026 main manuscript and its current Supplements S1-S3. This is the rework's source set. The earlier AlphaGenome paper and its five older supplements remain in the historical repository; they are not silently relabeled as this source set.

P01-P10 are first-pass lessons; R01-R10 are detailed main-manuscript readings; S01-S07 explain the current supplements. W01-W06 are synthetic worked examples; T01-T12 are practice with answers. INTRO identifies front matter and the evidence contract. The glossary and researcher workflow are additional teaching aids.

Completeness means an explicit treatment of every substantive source component: definitions, assumptions, proofs, counterexamples, both main tables, four main figures, both S3 tables, empirical methods/results, limitations and reproducibility/declaration scope. It does not mean reproducing every bibliography item or raw table cell, depositing the underlying maps, or certifying human learning. Figure coverage explains panels and their interpretation; the source research figures are not copied as genomic observations into this package. References are covered by their scientific roles and relevant priority boundaries.

The checksummed manifest records every source hash and each mapped excerpt hash. The optional source check verifies that every nonblank source line belongs to a mapped span. A line-coverage check is mechanical; scientific completeness additionally required a separate reading against all four frozen inputs. That model-assisted review found and corrected missing assumptions and a signed-versus-absolute ranking inequality. No human learner testing is claimed.

## Recheck the package

Run `python3 scripts/check_pdf_companions.py` to check the public editions, hashes, PDF structure signatures, teaching-unit inventory and coverage-row presence with the standard library. If the frozen four source files are available, add `--source-root path/to/proposed` to verify their bytes, excerpt hashes and complete nonblank-line coverage.

`python3 scripts/build_pdf_companions.py` regenerates the PDF layout from the Markdown editions. It requires ReportLab, pypdf, Pandoc and the documented Georgia/Arial fonts. After a content or renderer change, render and inspect the pages again before refreshing the checked artifact manifest; a successful renderer alone is not a visual review.
