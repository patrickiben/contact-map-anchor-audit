\section{Additional file 3: Monte Carlo
scope}\label{additional-file-3-monte-carlo-scope}

This supplement concerns the earlier Atlas profile audit. The spatial
fractional-credit tests use exact finite-label distributions and have no
Monte Carlo sampling error. The profile results below are retained
results; the additional checks dated 28 October 2026 are post-result
validation and do not replace the frozen analyses or their criteria.

The retained panel mean-statistic tests used 9,999 null draws and the
plus-one estimate (exceedances + 1)/10,000. The AVI missense
consequence-matched, context-weighted comparison, its analysis excluding
the chr13 genes, and the three other-intronic splicing comparisons each
had zero exceedances. Their raw reported p-value was therefore 0.0001,
the simulation resolution floor. The recorded plug-in Monte Carlo
standard error was 0.0001; this is an estimated precision diagnostic,
not a known true standard error at a zero-exceedance boundary. The
corresponding recorded pointwise 95\% Wilson interval for the exceedance
probability, mapped through the plus-one formula, was {[}0.000100,
0.000484{]}. These intervals describe Monte Carlo precision conditional
on the specified label-null model, not uncertainty in biological
effects.

unselected distances from the selected distance, then averages over
sites. Both highlighted missense means are negative even though they
exceed the means under their specified label nulls:

The simulated null means average 9,999 draws; they are not exact
conditional expectations. Significant enrichment here means a shift
relative to that specified null, not a positive average margin over the
strongest alternative. Source: retained

\nolinkurl{/families/primary|missense_matched_ctx/rows} and
\nolinkurl{/families/primary_nochr13|missense_matched_ctx/rows}, each
selecting the unique row with \texttt{scorer\ =\ AVI\_SCORE} and
\texttt{geometry\ =\ euclidean}; the columns are \texttt{statistic},
\texttt{null\_mean} and \texttt{excess\_over\_null}. These values also
appear in \nolinkurl{independent_math_results.json} under
\nolinkurl{/historical_uncertainty/rows/0} and
\nolinkurl{/historical_uncertainty/rows/1}.

The adjusted q-values are point estimates computed jointly within their
stated families. A raw-p standard error is not an adjusted-q standard
error. The printed q-values should not be interpreted as having exact
precision to every displayed digit. The exploratory CONTACT\_MAPS top-1
result, q = 0.0441, comes from a binomial test and does not use the
Monte Carlo mean-statistic calculation.

The frozen coding analysis reported 37 significant uniform-null
comparisons and 10 consequence-matched comparisons. Its planned
criterion was at most one quarter as many matched as uniform significant
comparisons; 10 exceeds 37/4, so that criterion was not met. Three
further independent random-number streams, each using 9,999 draws and
the unchanged statistics, sites, families and threshold, again produced
37 and 10. These redraws assess Monte Carlo seed sensitivity and do not
alter the original result.

A separate conservative check enclosed all 132 underlying exceedance
probabilities simultaneously using Bonferroni-adjusted, two-sided
Clopper--Pearson intervals with at least 95\% joint coverage. Mapping
through the plus-one formula and propagating lower and upper limits
monotonically through BH yielded significant-count ranges of 36--37 for
the uniform family and 8--11 for the matched family. These broad
enclosures do not establish either outcome of the quarter criterion. The
observed failure and its stability in the three redraws are therefore
reported together with the remaining Monte Carlo uncertainty. The
enclosures require independent identically distributed null draws within
a cell; dependence between cells does not invalidate the Bonferroni
union bound.

The analysis also changes eligibility: the matched coding comparison
excludes sites with fewer than two consequence-compatible alternatives.
Consequently, the difference between the two significant counts does not
isolate a null-model change at fixed positions. The prescribed mutation
weights are sensitivity assumptions and are not estimates of clinical
ascertainment probabilities.
